# ProfitPilot

ProfitPilot is a proposal economics calculator for agencies, consultants, and small service teams. It turns deal size, client spend, expected uplift, costs, and payback assumptions into a clear pricing recommendation, ROI summary, proposal preview, and sales email.

Live demo: https://incomparable-arithmetic-7153d2.netlify.app

## What it does

- Calculates minimum project price, profit margin, ROI, client upside, and payback period.
- Generates a client-facing proposal summary.
- Generates a sales email that can be copied, downloaded, or printed.
- Works as a lightweight lead magnet or paid micro-SaaS starter.

## Monetization idea

Package it as a paid calculator for agencies: free basic version, paid branded exports/templates, and custom calculators for niches.
